Explore with more context

One place to compare the details that matter.

Placement Atlas brings programme information and practical decision tools together, so applicants can investigate options in a consistent way.

01

Explore the programme catalogue

Browse and filter programmes by region, employer, training setting, salary, weekly hours and advertised Skilled Worker sponsorship information.

02

Understand locations

Use the interactive map and available site details to investigate where training may take place and consider the practicalities of travel.

03

Compare pay and hours

Review advertised annual pay alongside contracted hours, effective hourly comparisons and indicative take-home estimates with visible assumptions.

04

Personalise your comparisons

Set priorities such as pay, location, training setting and independence. Fit scores explain how listed programme details relate to those choices.

05

Build and organise a shortlist

Record decisions and notes, compare options side by side, reorder your shortlist and export or back up your work.

06

Practise and learn

Use Swipe Mode, the independent Oriel Simulation and the Learning Hub to explore decisions and understand the foundation training process.

Open, readable information

What the October 2026 Oriel catalogue can tell you.

Placement Atlas uses an Oriel CSV export supplied to the project on 7 November 2026. It contains 4,064 programmes in England and Wales. For visitors and search engines that cannot run the interactive app, we've published a separate, plain-HTML programme snapshot.

What is included in the static snapshot?

Programme totals for all 12 regions, median advertised gross salaries, counts marked “Yes” for Skilled Worker visa sponsorship and a clearly labelled programme example from each region. The figures are derived directly from the same 2026 catalogue as the app.

877 programmes are marked “Yes” for visa sponsorship in the snapshot, including 55 of 786 London programmes. These are historic advertisement fields, not live sponsorship approvals.

What it cannot confirm

Whether a programme is still available, whether sponsorship is currently possible for a particular applicant, the accuracy of an employer's description, or what day-to-day working conditions will be like. The data does not provide verified reviews or official offer predictions.

For eligibility and current programmes, consult NHS England's recruitment resources and the official Oriel listing. Read how to assess visa sponsorship.

Accounts and Google sign-in

Sign-in is optional. Your Google account is not used to browse the catalogue.

The main explorer works without an account. If you choose to sign in, the account features help keep your saved workspace available when you use another device.

What Google sign-in is used for

When you choose Continue with Google, Placement Atlas uses basic Google account identity information made available during sign-in, such as your account identifier, email address, name and profile picture if available. This is used to authenticate you, display your signed-in identity and associate your private saved workspace with your account.

Google sign-in is optional. You can browse and use the programme tools without connecting a Google account.

What Placement Atlas does not access

Placement Atlas does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or calendars. Google sign-in is for identity authentication, not permission to read those services.

Account authentication and optional cloud-workspace storage are provided through Supabase. The Privacy Policy explains the information processed, how saved workspaces are handled, the service providers involved and how to request account deletion.

How saved information works

Keep control of your planning workspace.

Without an account

Your programme decisions, filters, preferences and other supported workspace choices are saved in your current browser. They normally remain on that device unless you clear browser data or export them.

With an optional account

If you sign in and choose to save or import a workspace, supported choices can be synchronised to a private account record so you can restore your work on another device. See the Privacy Policy for details, including the information that may be included.

Data and limitations

Use the information as decision support, then verify the details.

The built-in catalogue is based on an October 2026 Oriel CSV export supplied to the project on 7 November 2026, containing 4,064 programmes across England and Wales; it is a dated snapshot, not a live vacancy feed. Programme descriptions, salary, hours, training sites, sponsorship information, eligibility and recruitment dates can change. Fit scores are generated from available programme fields and user-selected priorities; they are not verified workplace reviews or guarantees of culture, autonomy or workload. Map locations may not show every rotation site, and straight-line distance is not a journey time. Take-home estimates depend on stated tax, pension and student-loan assumptions. The Oriel Simulation is an independent practice tool, not the official Oriel allocation algorithm or a prediction of an offer. Always check current information with official Oriel and the relevant employer.
