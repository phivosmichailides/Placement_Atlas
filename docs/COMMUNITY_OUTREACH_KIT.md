# Placement Atlas — Community sharing brief (November 2026)

**Official site:** https://placementatlas.co.uk/  
**What it is:** An independent, free web-based comparison and shortlisting tool for applicants exploring Oriel foundation pharmacist training programmes across England and Wales.  
**Created and maintained by:** Phivos Michailides, a UCL MPharm student. This is an independent student-built project, **not** a university, NHS England, GPhC or Oriel service.

- **Purpose, limitations and creator:** https://placementatlas.co.uk/about.html

## Facts that can be quoted with proper context

- The maintained source is an **October 2026 Oriel CSV export supplied to the project on 7 November 2026**, with **4,064 programme listings in 12 England-and-Wales regions**.
- In that historic export, **877** programmes are marked **“Yes”** for Skilled Worker visa sponsorship; **55 of 786 London programmes** carry the same flag. These are *advertised source-data fields*, not confirmed current visa availability.
- The site lets applicants explore programme attributes including annual gross pay, weekly working hours, setting/rotations, geographic information and advertised sponsorship; they can compare and shortlist independently of their official Oriel application.
- The static snapshot page can be read **without JavaScript**; the interactive catalogue and personal workspace require it.
- The programme information, salaries and hours may change. Applicants must check official Oriel and employers before making decisions.

## Suggested short resource-directory description

> **Placement Atlas** is a free, independent tool for UK MPharm students comparing Oriel foundation pharmacist training programmes across England and Wales. It combines a dated 2026 catalogue with pay, hours, locations, training settings and sponsorship indicators, plus personal comparison and shortlisting tools. It does **not** replace Oriel or show live vacancies. https://placementatlas.co.uk/

## Good outreach practices

- Prioritise relevant audiences: MPharm course representatives, student pharmacy societies, educators and professional student-resource directories.
- Contact the person or team once with the most relevant link, then make at most a polite follow-up rather than sending repetitive reminders.
- Ask for an **editorially chosen resource mention**, not an artificial backlink or a false endorsement.
- Avoid presenting the historical sponsorship fields as live visa eligibility, or presenting independent tools as officially approved.
- Track real feedback and update the product where appropriate.
