What matters when comparing pharmacy foundation training placements?

1. Training setting and rotations

Look at whether a programme is hospital-based, general practice, community pharmacy, industry or split between settings. A multi-site programme may offer a different type of experience from a single-site role. Read each programme description, and verify the actual structure with the employer.

Allocated an industry / hospital / community split? Read our detailed industry foundation training guide for real-world examples of possible projects, the different industry departments, supervisor questions and routes into pharma careers.

2. Location and journeys

The training address on a listing may not represent every site where you will work. Check the locations of rotations, commuting options and how realistic the travel would be for your circumstances. Map markers and distances are helpful starting points, but they are not guarantees of journey times.

3. Salary and working hours

Compare the advertised salary and contracted weekly hours together, rather than relying only on annual pay. Listings can be incomplete or change; verify current terms and whether there are differences across rotation sites.

4. Your priorities and uncertainties

Record your preferences and questions as you explore. A programme that suits one applicant may not suit another. Pay, clinical exposure, development opportunities, location and personal circumstances deserve different weights for different people.

How Placement Atlas helps you organise your shortlist

  1. Explore: Browse the England and Wales catalogue and filter programmes by relevant fields such as region, setting, salary and hours.
  2. Map: View mapped locations and investigate sites, distance and transport context where the data is available.
  3. Personalise: Adjust the importance of different factors and inspect the reasons and trade-offs behind suggested fit scores. These scores are heuristics, not workplace reviews or official recommendations.
  4. Compare: Keep programmes side by side so that pay, location, description and training settings are easier to evaluate.
  5. Shortlist: Record decisions, notes and priorities, reorder candidates and export or back up the results. Oriel preferences are not changed by this tool.

How to compare Skilled Worker visa sponsorship in Oriel pharmacy programmes

Start with the Skilled worker visa field on the programme record, rather than assuming every employer or hospital sponsors applicants. In the October 2026 Placement Atlas export, 877 of 4,064 programmes are marked “Yes”, including 55 of 786 London programmes. See the full regional breakdown and use the interactive explorer to filter for the marked programmes.

An advertised “Yes” indicates what was entered into that dated export. It is not a promise of sponsorship, a valid job offer or an assessment of your immigration eligibility. A potential sponsor needs an appropriate licence, and pay, hours and other visa requirements may apply. NHS England explains employer sponsorship requirements in its foundation pharmacist employer FAQs.

  1. Check the advertised flag: filter for “Yes” in Placement Atlas, then open the official programme record in Oriel.
  2. Compare the job itself: read salary and weekly hours together, training settings, employer, and each rotation location.
  3. Verify with the employer: confirm whether sponsorship can actually be provided for this programme and recruitment round, and which conditions apply.
  4. Keep alternatives: record programmes that fit your priorities without treating a historic visa flag as a guaranteed outcome.

Official recruitment and visa rules are authoritative; Placement Atlas is an independent comparison aid and not an immigration adviser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare Oriel foundation pharmacist programmes effectively?

Start with training settings and the type of work you want, then compare annual gross salary and weekly hours together. Check every rotation's location, commute feasibility, dates and employer requirements. Consider whether the listing mentions supported development or study time. Finally, shortlist options and verify the latest details in official Oriel before ranking.

Which programmes offer Skilled Worker visa sponsorship?

In the project's October 2026 export, 877 programmes are marked “Yes” in the Skilled worker visa field. London has 55 such entries in its 786 listed programmes. The static regional breakdown shows the figures without JavaScript. Use the interactive sponsorship filter for the source listings and confirm current sponsorship directly with each employer.

Does “Skilled worker visa: Yes” guarantee sponsorship?

No. The field is an advertised programme attribute in a dated dataset. Sponsorship eligibility depends on the employer's licence, the role, relevant salary and hours thresholds, and the applicant's individual circumstances. Refer to NHS England's employer guidance and the current official listing.

What if one programme lists two or more postcodes?

Those may represent different rotation sites. Check the programme description for how time is divided and plan journeys for every site rather than judging the commute using a single postcode. A postcode pin or straight-line distance is not a verified journey-time estimate.

Are take-home salary estimates the same as actual pay?

No. The salary is a listed gross annual amount, while estimated net pay uses assumptions about tax and other deductions. Student loan, pension enrolment, personal circumstances and actual payroll arrangements can change take-home pay.

Can I read programme information without enabling JavaScript?

Yes. Our public 2026 programme snapshot provides HTML tables of regional programme counts, advertised visa sponsorship and median listed salaries, plus clearly labelled examples. The full personalised catalogue filtering, map and shortlisting still require the interactive app.

Is Placement Atlas the official Oriel website?

No. It is an independent decision-support application. Official applications, offers, rankings, deadlines and programme changes must be handled and checked through the official recruitment services.

Does the tool list every UK foundation pharmacist training programme?

No. Its built-in October 2026 snapshot covers England and Wales only. It is not a comprehensive database for every UK nation or future recruitment round.

Are the programmes and salaries updated live?

No. The embedded catalogue is based on a dated export. Information may change, and the site provides an option to upload a more recent programme CSV for your own use. Always check official programme information.

Can I use Placement Atlas to submit my Oriel preferences?

No. You can explore, compare, record and export your decisions, but submitting or changing preferences happens only through Oriel.

Is the personal fit score an objective quality rating?

No. It reflects programme data and user-selected priorities. It cannot confirm the day-to-day workload, working conditions, supervision quality or eligibility for a position.

Can I save my shortlist?

Yes. The app supports local saving and backups, with optional account-based synchronisation for supported data. Export your decisions and keep a backup before switching devices or clearing browser storage.
