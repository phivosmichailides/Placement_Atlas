Questions about the programme data

Which Oriel foundation pharmacist programmes offer Skilled Worker visa sponsorship?

In this October 2026 export, 877 of 4,064 programmes are marked “Yes” in the sponsorship field, including 55 of the 786 London entries. Use the interactive explorer's Skilled Worker visa filter to view the full flagged subset. A “Yes” is an advertisement in this dated source, not proof of current sponsorship eligibility or an issued visa.

Does a sponsorship flag guarantee a Skilled Worker visa?

No. The advertised flag indicates potential sponsorship, not an individual immigration decision. NHS England's employer guidance notes that the employer needs a suitable sponsor licence and that salary and working-hours requirements apply. Confirm the current programme and your circumstances through official sources.

Why compare annual salary and weekly hours together?

A higher gross annual salary may involve more contracted hours. Compare the advertised annual amount alongside weekly working hours, travel between sites and available study support. Placement Atlas can show estimated take-home pay, but tax, pension and other circumstances affect actual payments.

What are hospital, general practice, community and industry rotations?

These are training settings recorded in Oriel programme fields. A single programme can include several settings for different lengths of time. Review its description and official listing before assuming where you would work each day, and ask the employer when details are unclear.

Can I apply or update Oriel preferences on this website?

No. Placement Atlas is an independent, optional comparison and shortlisting workspace. It does not transmit your choices to Oriel. Official applications, rankings and offer responses happen in Oriel.

Where are the remaining programme details?

The interactive explorer provides searchable programme names, employers, sector fields, salaries, hours, sponsorship flags, location context and decision tools using the full dated source. This static summary deliberately publishes useful aggregates and a small set of attributed examples rather than duplicating thousands of potentially stale listings.
